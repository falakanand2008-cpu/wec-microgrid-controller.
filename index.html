How the controller decides

  1. Solar first. It's free (up to 200 units/hour) and goes to every building except lecture halls, which can't take solar.
  2. Battery next, lecture halls first. The battery is spent only when it saves money: the grid costs more than $1, the grid has failed, or it's the last hour.
  3. The grid covers the rest.
  4. The generator ($1/unit) keeps the battery full as grid-failure insurance. When the grid costs more than $1, we spend the battery and refill it, saving (price − $1) on every unit.
